About

Built for how campus life actually works

Students already juggle a campus feed, a group chat for their org, a spreadsheet for dues, and a family tree drawn on a whiteboard. Chirp puts those in one app instead of four.

What we believe

Chirp is for every student

Greek life is one part of campus, not the default identity of every user. Every student gets the same Home feed and Yak; chapters and student orgs get extra tools layered on top, not a separate app.

Money is boring on purpose

The dues ledger is append-only — corrections are new entries, not edits, so the record can't be quietly rewritten. Dues run through Stripe with the chapter as merchant of record, so chapters aren't trusting an app with their bank account.

Anonymous means anonymous — to your peers

Yak hides who you are from other students. It doesn't pretend accountability disappears entirely: reports and moderation still operate behind the scenes to keep the board usable.

Where we are

Still building, on purpose

Chirp is in active development. The campus feed, Yak, chapter member and role tools, the treasurer and secretary dashboards, dues collection, and the alumni network are built. Direct messaging and the interactive family-tree view are still in progress — see Features for the current split. The app isn't on the App Store or Google Play yet.

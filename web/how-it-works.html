Bring Chirp to your chapter

How it works

From sign-up to dues, step by step

1

Create your account

Sign up with your email. During setup you tell Chirp who you are: a student, someone in a fraternity or sorority, or an alum.

2

Land on your campus

Your Home feed and Yak are scoped to your campus from the start — no setup required beyond picking your school.

3

Join your chapter (optional)

An officer shares an invite link — the kind this site hands off at /join-chapter — or you paste the invite code directly in the app. Joining adds your chapter to your Orgs tab.

4

Get the tools your role needs

Every member sees the chapter feed, events, and member directory. Treasurers and presidents additionally see the dues dashboard; secretaries and presidents see meeting minutes and attendance.

Dues

How dues actually move

This is the part worth being precise about, because it's real money.

1

The chapter connects its own Stripe account

A treasurer or president starts Stripe Connect onboarding — a Stripe-hosted Express account for the chapter, not for Chirp.

2

Stripe verifies the chapter

Once Stripe confirms the account can accept charges, the chapter's dues cycles become payable to members.

3

Members pay by card or bank transfer

Each payment is a Stripe PaymentIntent created directly on the chapter's connected account — a direct charge, not a transfer through Chirp.

4

Money settles to the chapter, not to Chirp

Funds land in the chapter's own Stripe balance. Chirp takes a platform fee — 1% on card payments, 2% on bank transfers — and never takes custody of the money at any point.

5

Every payment hits an append-only ledger

Dues payments, expenses, and any corrections are recorded as new entries, never edits — so the chapter's financial record can't be quietly rewritten.

Card data never touches Chirp's servers. Payment details go straight from your device to Stripe.

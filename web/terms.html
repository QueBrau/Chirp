Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026. Draft — see "Not yet reviewed" below.

These terms have not been reviewed by a lawyer. They plainly describe how Chirp works and what we expect from users today. They are not a finished, legally binding contract, and Chirp should not launch publicly on the strength of this page alone. See "What still needs legal review" near the bottom.

What Chirp is

Chirp is a social and operations app for college students — a campus feed and an anonymous board (Yak) for everyone, plus chapter tools (dues, a family tree, meeting minutes, role-based dashboards) for fraternities, sororities, and other student orgs. It's currently in development and not yet available on the App Store or Google Play.

Accounts

  • You need an account to use Chirp, created with your email and a password today (Google and Apple sign-in are planned, not yet functional).
  • You're responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for activity that happens under your account.
  • Chapter roles (member, treasurer, president, secretary, historian, and others) are assigned by your chapter's existing officers or by joining through an invite. Roles control which chapter tools you can see and use.

Content and conduct

  • You own what you post. By posting to the Home feed, your chapter feed, or Yak, you give Chirp the ability to store and display it back to the audience that content is intended for (your chapter, your campus, or the public feed, depending on where you post it).
  • Don't harass, threaten, or dox other users — including on Yak. Posts on Yak don't show your name to other students, but Chirp can still act on reports and link a post back to the account that made it for moderation and enforcement.
  • We can act on content that violates these rules. What actually works today: reporting and blocking on Yak, and removal of content by moderators through our backend. Broader in-app moderation tools, and account-level suspension or banning, are not built yet — we're stating what exists rather than what we intend to build.
  • Don't attempt to identify who's behind an anonymous Yak post through the app or by other means to harass them.

Chapter tools

If your chapter uses Chirp's family tree, treasurer, or secretary tools, the people your chapter has given those roles to can see and manage records like lineage, dues, and meeting minutes for your chapter. Chirp itself doesn't referee chapter governance disputes — that's on the chapter.

Dues and payments

  • Dues are collected through Stripe. Your chapter connects its own Stripe account (a Stripe Express account) and is the merchant of record for its own dues payments — Chirp is not a party to that transaction and never takes custody of the funds.
  • Chirp takes a platform fee on each dues payment (currently 1% on card payments, 2% on bank transfers) as compensation for providing the payment infrastructure.
  • Refunds, disputes, and chargebacks on a dues payment are between the paying member and the chapter's Stripe account, not Chirp.
  • Card and bank account details are entered directly into Stripe's interface and never pass through Chirp's servers.

Termination

You can stop using Chirp at any time. We can suspend or terminate an account that violates these terms, including the conduct rules above. We haven't yet documented a formal notice or appeal process for account actions — see below.

No warranty, as things stand

Chirp is under active development. Parts of it (see Features) are still being built, and things will break. We're not making uptime or reliability promises at this stage.

What still needs legal review

These terms deliberately stop short of language that needs a lawyer, rather than guessing at it:

  • Limitation of liability and disclaimer of warranties — not yet drafted in enforceable legal language.
  • Indemnification.
  • Governing law and dispute resolution (including whether arbitration applies).
  • Formal age/eligibility requirements and any school-affiliation verification.
  • A defined process and timeline for account suspension notice and appeal.
  • Intellectual property / DMCA takedown process and designated agent.

Changes to these terms

We'll update this page as the product changes. There isn't a formal versioning or notice process for changes yet — that's part of the legal review above.

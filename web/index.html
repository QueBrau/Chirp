What's inside

Everything your campus life actually runs on

Home feed

Text, photo, and video posts. Switch between For You and Campus to see what your school is talking about.

Yak

An anonymous board for your whole campus. Your posts don't show who you are to other students — reports and moderation keep it in check.

Chapter tools

For fraternities, sororities, and student orgs: dues collection, a family tree, meeting minutes, and role-based dashboards for your e-board.

Dues

Your chapter is the merchant of record.

Dues run through Stripe Connect. Your treasurer sets up your chapter's own Stripe account, members pay by card or bank transfer, and the money settles directly to your chapter. Chirp never takes custody of the funds.

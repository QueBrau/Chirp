What Chirp does today

Chirp serves every student, not just Greek life. Everyone gets the campus feed and Yak; chapters and student orgs get tools to actually run themselves underneath.

For every student

Home feed

Post text, photos, or video. Filter between For You and Campus to see a mix of what's relevant to you or everything happening at your school. Like and comment on posts.

Yak — the anonymous board

A campus-wide board where posts don't carry your name to other students. Upvote and downvote what resonates. Reports and moderation run behind the scenes so the board stays usable — your identity is never shown to other students, though Chirp retains the ability to act on reports and enforce bans.

For chapters and student orgs

If you're in a fraternity, sorority, or another student org, your org gets its own space inside Chirp with role-gated tools for the people running it.

Members & roles

A member directory with roles — president, vice president, treasurer, secretary, historian, and more. Tools show up for the people who hold the relevant role.

Treasurer & dues

Dues cycles, an append-only ledger (corrections instead of edits, so the record stays honest), and dues collection by card or bank transfer through Stripe Connect. Your chapter is the merchant of record — see how dues work.

Secretary

Meeting minutes and attendance tracking, kept with the rest of your chapter's records.

Invite-code joining

Officers generate an invite link; new members open it and join the chapter roster directly — no paperwork.

Alumni network & jobs

Alumni profiles (grad year, company, industry) and a job board — posts can come from a specific chapter or the wider network.

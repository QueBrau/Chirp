## Operational evidence and limits

- **Proven:** existing c369/c69 evidence records daily apply for 30-day expired posts/comments/Chirps and 7-day eligible prekey retirement.
- **Analytics boundary:** the sink and dataset are documented in `ANALYTICS-VERIFICATION.md`; the accepted schemas intentionally include joinable UUIDs and lifecycle/payment dimensions. This is product usage measurement, not merely operational logging. The evidence documents a synthetic sink probe, but does not prove complete deployed API/WS collection or a dataset expiry setting.
- **Not proven:** media object deletion (c414 remains dry-run), provider backup expiration, Cloud Logging retention, Firebase/Stripe retention settings, account deletion, account-wide export, message deletion, or fulfillment of a legal request.
- **Dormant/planned client storage:** the mobile SQLite decrypted-message schema and plaintext outbox are present but have no current callers. They should not be described as current shipped collection without a separate release/build check.
- **Public claims requiring reconciliation:** push is currently no-op on both relevant paths; `social.ts` static availability flags do not override the actual Apple/Google platform availability functions; public copy should remain transport-only/no E2EE. Photo final derivatives strip EXIF, while temporary source uploads may retain it. Public policy should disclose device/session persistence, signed media uploads, OS cache/share behavior, and the absence of self-service account deletion/export.
- **Retention wording:** use “eligible for deletion after 30 days” for content and “eligible prekey retirement after 7 days” as supported by the accepted evidence. Do not add exact backup or log retention periods without provider evidence.

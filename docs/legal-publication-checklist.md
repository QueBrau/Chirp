### Proposed support procedure for review

Ordinary privacy requests: record the received time and requested scope; verify using the least necessary information; restrict access to the case; identify relevant systems and any specific retention obligation; arrange secure delivery/correction/deletion; reply within the existing 30-day commitment or a shorter applicable limit. Explain incomplete work and retained categories without calling an acknowledgement completed fulfillment. Record the outcome and any appeal. A separate data-retention schedule is still needed for case records.

Urgent image requests: route immediately to the designated primary and backup; identify whether the four notice elements are present without adding police/court/ID requirements; preserve only necessary metadata through the approved process; locate and disable access to the reported image across Chirp-controlled surfaces, act on known identical copies, and document timing and outcome. The statutory window is not the ordinary support SLA. Do not use an inability to staff the mailbox to postpone applicable duties. This procedure is proposed, not verified or deployed; c437 owns the working process.

## Primary sources checked for this draft

- [TAKE IT DOWN Act, Public Law 119-12, section 3](https://www.congress.gov/119/plaws/publ12/PLAW-119publ12.pdf): covered-platform written notice process; signature, sufficient identification/location, good-faith statement and contact details; removal and reasonable efforts for known identical copies within 48 hours of a valid request. Chirp's UGC functions warrant applicability review. This is not a determination by counsel.
- [FTC TAKE IT DOWN guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act): practical process guidance. A public email address is not proof of response or removal capability.
- [Apple App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), sections 1.2 and 5.1.1, and [account deletion guidance](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion): UGC controls, privacy and in-app deletion initiation. Email-only deletion instructions do not establish acceptance.
- [Google account deletion](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111), [UGC](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9876937), and [child safety FAQ](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14747720): actual controls and operational contacts are separate from public rules.
- [Google age-restricted content policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/18258653?hl=en-GB) and [policy deadlines](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/table/12921780?hl=en): assess the anonymous-communication classification and minors restrictions effective August 26, 2026. This is an applicability question about Chirp's functionality, not a conclusion that every campus board is prohibited.
- [Stripe service terms](https://stripe.com/legal/ssa-service-terms), Connect section 3.4, and [Connected Account Agreement](https://stripe.com/legal/connect-account): describe and obtain the relevant organization authority, activity and data-sharing acceptance. The actual direct-charge integration, not generic Stripe capabilities, controls the draft's description.
- [FTC on retroactive privacy changes](https://www.ftc.gov/policy/advocacy-research/tech-at-ftc/2024/02/ai-other-companies-quietly-changing-your-terms-service-could-be-unfair-or-deceptive): updating a notice does not silently permit inconsistent new data uses.
- [California AG privacy guidance](https://oag.ca.gov/privacy/ccpa), [education-record definition](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/faq/what-education-record), and [Copyright Office section 512](https://www.copyright.gov/512/index.html): counsel must assess actual applicability; do not claim blanket exemptions or registered-agent/safe-harbor status.
